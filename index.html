07Questions

What homeowners ask us.

Do you handle approvals?

Yes. For structural changes we prepare drawings and handle approvals with your society (DHA, Bahria, Askari) or LDA where required. [Confirm for your area]

Can we live in the house during the work?

For a full house renovation, most families move out for part of the work. We plan it in phases, one floor at a time, so you can stay if you prefer.

What happens if I change my mind mid-project?

You get a written change order with the price and the days it adds. Work on that item starts after you sign it.

How do payments work?

20% advance to book materials and start, then payments tied to finished stages. The last 10% is due after the final walkthrough.

What if the project runs late?

We tell you the same day and say why. If the delay is ours, the contract credits you for it.

What does the warranty cover?

Our labor and workmanship for 5 years, in writing. Manufacturer warranties cover products.
